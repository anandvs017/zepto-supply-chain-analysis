# Zepto Supply Chain Analytics — DRHP Deep Dive

A data analysis project built from Zepto's official UDRHP-1 filing with SEBI (June 8, 2026), ahead of its expected July 2026 IPO on NSE and BSE.

Zepto will be India's first pure-play quick commerce company to list publicly. This project asks one question: **what do the supply chain numbers actually say about whether this business is ready?**

---

## The three questions this project answers

1. Is Zepto's dark store network becoming more efficient over time, or just bigger?
2. How does Zepto's unit economics compare to Blinkit and Swiggy Instamart?
3. What is the realistic path to store-level breakeven?

---

## Key findings

**Finding 1: The densification strategy is working**
Orders per day per store jumped 50% in a single year — from 1,425 in Q4 FY25 to 2,140 in Q4 FY26. This is the most important supply chain metric in the DRHP. It means fixed store costs are being spread across significantly more orders, which directly improves contribution margins without requiring new capital.
